## 2. THE SUBJECT ZERO EQUATION

The entire protocol is built on a single equation:

**R = (S × F × I) / T**

Where:

| Symbol | Meaning | Brain Component |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R** | Regulation / Resulting Reality | The life you experience day to day |
| **S** | Soothing (Amygdala calm) | The Guard at the gate |
| **F** | Filter (RAS retuning) | The Door of Perception |
| **I** | Installation (Subconscious code) | The Operating System |
| **T** | Tension / Trigger / Trauma Resistance | The active mind, the old loop, the noise |

### The Expanded Formula

**O = A × (S × F × I) / T + SC**

Where:

| Symbol | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **F** | Filter |

- When Soothing, Filter, and Installation are high, and Tension is low, R becomes high.
- The superconscious is not a variable. It is the space beyond the equation.

**The firewall holds:** The protocol does not attract outcomes. It changes what you see, and action bridges.
