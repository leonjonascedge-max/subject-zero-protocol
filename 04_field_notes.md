# 04. Field Notes

## Origin Story

Day Zero began with tea, an empty cupboard, and one question about the brain.

The first script was built that day. From there, the protocol became a public experiment: a living study in how nervous system regulation, language, and daily action could reshape a person’s life.

This project was never designed as an abstract theory. It was built from direct experience, iteration, and field observation.

## The 90-Day Recap

The first 90 days established the framework and the proof layer:

- Foundation scripts were built and refined
- Two-loop structure was corrected and finalized
- Somatic Anchor V2.0 was adopted
- Payment and distribution infrastructure was created
- The product ladder was clarified
- Launch milestones were reached

The work moved from hypothesis to structure.

## Movement Status

### Individuals enrolled

- Subject 01: enrolled, early scripts completed, reported positive shifts
- Subject 02: enrolled, focused on amygdala regulation through a 7-day cycle

### Observed reality

Personalized audios are being compiled for interested individuals. One major operational challenge remains: many interested people do not have reliable smartphone access. The app must solve this problem.

## Phase 2 Focus

Phase 2 is the build-and-yield stage.

Current focus:

- integrate the protocol as a lifestyle, not as a temporary experiment
- scale the digital products and QES
- shift from storytelling to harvesting
- compile the 90-day log into a book

## Key Decisions

- The $17.99 product is the complete audio toolkit, not just script text.
- All scripts remain free in weekly drops; the paid path is convenience and structure.
- The OS Map remains one of the core operational tools.
- Telegram is the house; TikTok is the discovery engine.
- YouTube is permanently banned from the distribution stack.
- The protocol changes the perceiver, not the perceived.
- Script 7 branches by loop: Agency for the morning, Gratitude for the evening.

## Important Field Observations

- The guard is not the enemy. Soothe, don't bypass.
- The body must be regulated before language can install deeply.
- The protocol is a nervous system system, not a manifestation system.
- Action is the bridge between internal shift and external result.
- The real work is not persuasive language; it is consistent embodiment.

## Final Position

The protocol is no longer an experiment in isolation. It is becoming a public operating system for regulation, action, and field-building.

The immediate next step is not more theory. It is consistent execution, sharper distribution, and visible proof.
