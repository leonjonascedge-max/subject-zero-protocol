# 02. Protocol

## The Somatic Anchor V2.0

**Status:** Major upgrade, live as of the Phase 1 → Phase 2 transition.

The amygdala is pre-verbal. It does not understand language alone; it understands physical evidence.

That means words alone cannot soothe the guard. The body must go first.

### Somatic Anchor standard

Every audio must be preceded by a physical grounding sequence:

- 5-senses check-in
- 4-8 breathing pattern
- 4 counts in, 8 counts out

This is non-negotiable. It is the physical evidence the amygdala requires before language can do installation work.

## Audio Specs

- Intros: 0.85x speed
- Body text: 0.64x speed
- Gap between lines: 3 seconds
- Export format: MP3 only

## Embodiment V3.0

Identity statements are upgraded to embodied language examples such as:

- "I am the man who delivers undeniable value."
- "I am the man who receives abundance with ease."

These are meant to be felt, not just repeated.

## The Foundation Series

The Foundation Series is six shared scripts plus one branching closer. It is not eight independent scripts in a flat sequence.

### Shared healing scripts

1. Amygdala Soothing Reset — calm the guard, signal safety
2. Limiting Contract Revocation — break the old vow, null the old contract
3. Money-Safety — neutralize money fear, receiving and spending safely
4. Body Rebuilding — treat the body as the mission carrier
5. Professional Edge — install verified competence
6. The Root Script — heal the deepest wound, universalized from personal origin

### Script 7 — The Branching Closer

Script 7 takes two forms depending on the loop.

- **Morning Loop Closer — Agency Activation**
  - moves the person from repair to action
- **Evening Loop Closer — Gratitude**
  - closes the day, releases weight, supports sleep and integration

Never combine both in one loop.

---

## The Two-Loop Structure

Both loops begin with Amygdala Soothing Reset and share Scripts 1–6. They branch only at Script 7.

### Morning / Day Loop

- Function: activation
- Purpose: prime the nervous system for movement and daily action
- Closer: Agency Activation

### Evening / Night Loop

- Function: integration
- Purpose: close the day, release the tension, support rest
- Closer: Gratitude

**Why agency is excluded from the evening loop:** agency is a move signal. It is not the correct signal for sleep. Gratitude is used as the release signal instead.

## Ascension Series

The Ascension Series is not a separate product in theory; it is the second movement of the same journey.

It sits after the Foundation and is structured as a premium continuation, not a competing system.

### Decision framework

The working model is:

- Foundation first
- Ascension second
- Product ladder follows naturally

This preserves order and avoids overloading a person before the nervous system has stabilized.

---

## Operating Principle

The protocol is not about forcing a person to feel better. It is about creating the conditions under which the nervous system can regulate more effectively and the person can act from stability rather than stress.

This is why embedded breath, physical grounding, and repeated loops matter more than clever language alone.
